## Overview
The kuhli loach is a slender, eel-like fish with bands of dark and light color. It comes from Southeast Asia, where it lives in shallow streams and burrows into soft sand. In the home aquarium it is a calm, interesting bottom dweller that suits a community tank.

Kuhli loaches are social and do best in a group of at least six. They are nocturnal, so they are most active at night, and they often hide during the day. Give them soft sand to burrow in and plenty of hiding places.

## Tank setup
A group needs a tank of at least 20 gallons (76 L). Keep the water between 75 and 86 °F (24–30 °C) with a pH of 5.5 to 7.0. Use soft sand for the bottom, since they burrow and can scratch themselves on sharp gravel. Provide caves, driftwood, and plants for cover, and keep the lighting dim. Gentle flow works best. Use a secure lid, because loaches are known to explore and escape.

## Feeding
Kuhli loaches are omnivores. Feed sinking pellets, wafers, or frozen foods such as bloodworms in the evening, when they are most active. Make sure food reaches the bottom so that every fish gets some.

## Common mistakes
- Keeping fewer than six, which leaves them shy and hidden.
- Using sharp or coarse gravel that can injure their delicate bodies while they burrow.
- Keeping a bright, open tank with no hides, which stresses a nocturnal fish.
- Feeding only during the day, when most food is missed or eaten by faster fish.
