## Overview
The marbled hatchetfish is a small, deep-bodied surface fish from South America. It has a hatchet-shaped body and a mottled pattern that makes it easy to recognize. In the home aquarium it is a fascinating fish to watch, because it spends most of its time near the top of the tank.

Marbled hatchetfish are peaceful and shy, and they do best in a group of at least six. They are strong jumpers, so a tight lid is essential. They are a rewarding choice for a beginner who wants a calm fish that stays near the surface.

## Tank setup
A group needs a tank of at least 20 gallons (76 L). Keep the water between 75 and 82 °F (24–28 °C) with a pH of 5.5 to 7.5. Provide plants near the surface and floating plants for cover, and keep open swimming space at the top. Gentle flow is best, since these fish prefer calm water. A tight-fitting lid is essential, and standard lighting is fine.

## Feeding
Marbled hatchetfish are omnivores that feed at the surface. Offer floating flakes or small pellets, and add small frozen or live foods such as brine shrimp or daphnia. Feed once or twice a day in small portions, and make sure food reaches the top of the tank.

## Common mistakes
- Using a tank without a tight lid, which invites a jump and a fatal fall.
- Keeping fewer than six, which leaves them shy and stressed.
- Housing them with boisterous or fast fish, which makes these shy fish hide and decline.
- Dropping food only to the bottom, when hatchetfish feed at the surface.
