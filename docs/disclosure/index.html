Affiliate disclosure and privacy

Tankmate Check is free to use. Some links go to Amazon. As an Amazon Associate this site earns from qualifying purchases, at no extra cost to you. Those links never affect a compatibility verdict, which comes only from the species data.

This site sets no cookies of its own and runs no ads. Amazon may set cookies when you follow a link to its site; see Amazon's own privacy notice for details. The site host may keep standard server logs.

Tankmate Check checks freshwater aquarium compatibility from each species' temperature, pH, size, temperament and diet. It is a starting point, not a guarantee: individual fish vary, so watch new tankmates closely.
