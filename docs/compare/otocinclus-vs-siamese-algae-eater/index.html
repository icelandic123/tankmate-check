Otocinclus vs Siamese Algae Eater: Which Should You Buy?

Pick the otocinclus if

  • you have a smaller tank: it needs 15 gallons (57 L) against 30 gallons (114 L).
  • your tank runs cooler: it is comfortable down to 70 °F (21 °C).
  • your water is soft or acidic (it handles pH down to 6).
  • you want more tankmate options: 38 of the 51 species we cover are a safe match, against 35.
  • you keep or plan to keep shrimp.
  • you're stocking a small or nano tank (1.6 in (4 cm) adult size).

Pick the siamese algae eater if

  • you want an easier fish (easy care, against moderate).
  • your tap water is hard or alkaline (it handles pH up to 8).
  • you want a bigger, more visible animal (5.9 in (15 cm) against 1.6 in (4 cm)).
  • you want fewer animals: one or a pair instead of 6+.
