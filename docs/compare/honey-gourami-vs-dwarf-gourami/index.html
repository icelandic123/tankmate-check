Honey Gourami vs Dwarf Gourami: Which Should You Buy?

Bottom line: The honey gourami is gentler, smaller and fits a 10-gallon. The dwarf gourami is more colorful but territorial and prone to a viral disease, so buy it only from a careful source.

Pick the honey gourami if

  • you have a smaller tank: it needs 10 gallons (38 L) against 15 gallons (57 L).
  • you want an easier fish (easy care, against moderate).
  • your tank runs cooler: it is comfortable down to 72 °F (22 °C).
  • you want more tankmate options: 37 of the 51 species we cover are a safe match, against 28.
  • you're stocking a small or nano tank (2 in (5 cm) adult size).

Pick the dwarf gourami if

  • you want a bigger, more visible animal (3.5 in (9 cm) against 2 in (5 cm)).

Can you keep both?

With caution Male dwarf gouramis can bully the smaller honey gourami.
