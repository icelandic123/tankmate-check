Bronze Corydoras vs Panda Corydoras: Which Should You Buy?

Bottom line: Bronze cories are hardier and tolerate a wider range of water; pandas are smaller and prettier but prefer cooler water and are more sensitive. For a first tank, bronze.

Pick the bronze corydoras if

  • you want an easier fish (easy care, against moderate).
  • your tap water is hard or alkaline (it handles pH up to 8).
  • you want more tankmate options: 35 of the 51 species we cover are a safe match, against 25.

Pick the panda corydoras if

  • you have a smaller tank: it needs 15 gallons (57 L) against 20 gallons (76 L).
  • your tank runs cooler: it is comfortable down to 68 °F (20 °C).
