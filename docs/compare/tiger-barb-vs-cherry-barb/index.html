Tiger Barb vs Cherry Barb: Which Should You Buy?

Bottom line: Cherry barbs are calm community fish. Tiger barbs are boisterous fin nippers that need a bigger group and tankmates without long fins. For a mixed community, cherry barbs.

Pick the cherry barb if

  • you have a smaller tank: it needs 15 gallons (57 L) against 30 gallons (114 L).
  • you want more tankmate options: 39 of the 51 species we cover are a safe match, against 33.
