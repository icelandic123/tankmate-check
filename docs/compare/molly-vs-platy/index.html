Molly vs Platy: Which Should You Buy?

Bottom line: Platies are smaller, hardier and happy in 15 gallons and cooler water. Mollies get bigger, need warmer, harder water and at least 30 gallons. For most beginners, platies.

Pick the molly if

  • your tank runs warm: it is comfortable up to 82 °F (28 °C).
  • your tap water is hard or alkaline (it handles pH up to 8.5).
  • you want a bigger, more visible animal (3.9 in (10 cm) against 2.4 in (6 cm)).

Pick the platy if

  • you have a smaller tank: it needs 15 gallons (57 L) against 30 gallons (114 L).
  • your tank runs cooler: it is comfortable down to 68 °F (20 °C).
  • you're stocking a small or nano tank (2.4 in (6 cm) adult size).

Can you keep both?

Yes Their needs overlap, so you could keep both.
