Cherry Shrimp vs Amano Shrimp: Which Should You Buy?

Bottom line: Cherry shrimp are colorful and breed into a colony; Amanos are bigger, eat more algae and survive more tankmates, but never breed in fresh water. For algae, Amanos; for a colony, cherries.

Pick the cherry shrimp if

  • you have a smaller tank: it needs 5 gallons (19 L) against 10 gallons (38 L).
  • you're stocking a small or nano tank (1.2 in (3 cm) adult size).

Pick the amano shrimp if

  • your water is soft or acidic (it handles pH down to 6).
  • you want a bigger, more visible animal (2 in (5 cm) against 1.2 in (3 cm)).
  • you want fewer animals: 3 instead of 10+.

Can you keep both?

Yes Their needs overlap, so you could keep both.
