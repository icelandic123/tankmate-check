Neon Tetra vs Cardinal Tetra: Which Should You Buy?

Bottom line: Cardinals are bigger, redder and like warmer, softer water; neons are cheaper, tolerate cooler tanks and fit a 10-gallon. In a warm, soft-water planted tank, cardinals look better; in a cooler tank or a 10-gallon, neons are the right buy.

Pick the neon tetra if

  • you have a smaller tank: it needs 10 gallons (38 L) against 20 gallons (76 L).
  • your tank runs cooler: it is comfortable down to 68 °F (20 °C).

Pick the cardinal tetra if

  • your tank runs warm: it is comfortable up to 84 °F (29 °C).
  • your water is soft or acidic (it handles pH down to 4.5).

Can you keep both?

Yes Their needs overlap, so you could keep both.
