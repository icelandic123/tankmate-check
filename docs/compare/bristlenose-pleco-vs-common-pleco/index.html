Bristlenose Pleco vs Common Pleco: Which Should You Buy?

Bottom line: Buy the bristlenose. It does the same algae job and stays around 5 inches, while a common pleco grows past 18 inches and needs a tank over 150 gallons.

Pick the bristlenose pleco if

  • you have a smaller tank: it needs 30 gallons (114 L) against 151 gallons (570 L).
  • you want an easier fish (easy care, against moderate).
  • you're stocking a small or nano tank (4.7 in (12 cm) adult size).

Pick the common pleco if

  • your tap water is hard or alkaline (it handles pH up to 7.8).
  • you want a bigger, more visible animal (17.7 in (45 cm) against 4.7 in (12 cm)).

Can you keep both?

With caution Both fish claim territory on the bottom; give each its own cave and plenty of floor space.
