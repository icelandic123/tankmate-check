Nerite Snail vs Mystery Snail: Which Should You Buy?

Bottom line: Nerites are the stronger algae eaters and never overrun a tank, but they leave hard white eggs. Mystery snails are bigger and more fun to watch, but fin nippers pick at their feelers.

Pick the nerite snail if

  • your tap water is hard or alkaline (it handles pH up to 8.5).
  • you want more tankmate options: 42 of the 51 species we cover are a safe match, against 39.
  • you're stocking a small or nano tank (1 in (2.5 cm) adult size).

Pick the mystery snail if

  • your tank runs cooler: it is comfortable down to 68 °F (20 °C).
  • you want a bigger, more visible animal (2 in (5 cm) against 1 in (2.5 cm)).
