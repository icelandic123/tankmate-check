German Blue Ram vs Bolivian Ram: Which Should You Buy?

Bottom line: The German blue ram is more colorful but needs very warm, soft, spotless water and is often short-lived in average tanks. The Bolivian ram is the better first dwarf cichlid.

Pick the german blue ram if

  • you have a smaller tank: it needs 20 gallons (76 L) against 30 gallons (114 L).
  • your tank runs warm: it is comfortable up to 86 °F (30 °C).
  • your water is soft or acidic (it handles pH down to 5).

Pick the bolivian ram if

  • you want an easier fish (easy care, against hard).
  • your tank runs cooler: it is comfortable down to 72 °F (22 °C).
  • your tap water is hard or alkaline (it handles pH up to 7.5).
  • you want more tankmate options: 31 of the 51 species we cover are a safe match, against 6.

Can you keep both?

No No shared temperature: German Blue Ram needs 27–30 °C, Bolivian Ram needs 22–26 °C.
