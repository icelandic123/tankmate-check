Angelfish vs Discus: Which Should You Buy?

Bottom line: Angelfish are the practical choice: hardier and fine at normal tropical temperatures. Discus need water above 82 °F and spotless conditions, which few tankmates or keepers manage.

Pick the angelfish if

  • you have a smaller tank: it needs 55 gallons (208 L) against 75 gallons (284 L).
  • you want an easier fish (moderate care, against hard).
  • your tank runs cooler: it is comfortable down to 75 °F (24 °C).
  • your tap water is hard or alkaline (it handles pH up to 7.5).
  • you want more tankmate options: 25 of the 51 species we cover are a safe match, against 4.
  • you want fewer animals: one or a pair instead of 5+.

Pick the discus if

  • your water is soft or acidic (it handles pH down to 5).
