Best Tank Mates for Oscars

The oscar does best in a tank of its own kind. Eats any fish it can swallow and produces heavy waste. Tankmates must be large and robust.

Adult size13 in (33 cm)
Minimum tank75 gallons (284 L)

pH6–8
TemperamentAggressive

Best tank mates for a oscar

None of the species we cover are a safe match. Keep it with its own kind.

Possible, with caution

  • AngelfishA Angelfish is close to bite-size for an adult Oscar; young or small individuals are at risk.
  • Bristlenose PlecoAn adult bristlenose is armored and usually ignored by an oscar, but a small one can be eaten. Add it only fully grown and give it caves the oscar can't reach.
  • Common PlecoCommon plecos are one of the few fish regularly kept with oscars: armored, fast-growing and too big to swallow once adult. Both are huge waste producers, so the tank needs to be far larger than either needs alone.
  • Rainbow SharkA Rainbow Shark is close to bite-size for an adult Oscar; young or small individuals are at risk.
  • Red Tail SharkA Red Tail Shark is close to bite-size for an adult Oscar; young or small individuals are at risk.
