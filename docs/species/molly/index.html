Best Tank Mates for Mollies

Good tank mates for a molly include angelfishs, betta fishs, bristlenose plecos, bronze corydoras. Prefers hard, alkaline water; struggles in soft, acidic tanks.

Possible, with caution

  • African Dwarf FrogThe African Dwarf Frog is a slow eater; the faster Molly will take most of the food unless you target-feed.
  • Cherry ShrimpAdult cherry shrimp are usually safe, but the Molly will eat baby shrimp, so the colony won't grow much without dense plants or moss.
  • Fancy GoldfishTheir temperature ranges barely overlap (24–24 °C), so you'd keep one of them at the edge of its comfort zone.

Avoid

  • AxolotlNo shared temperature: Molly needs 24–28 °C, Axolotl needs 16–20 °C.
  • OscarAn adult Oscar can swallow a Molly (about 10 cm), and will.
  • White Cloud Mountain MinnowNo shared temperature: Molly needs 24–28 °C, White Cloud Mountain Minnow needs 14–22 °C.
  • Convict CichlidThe Convict Cichlid is aggressive and will bully or kill a peaceful Molly.
  • Pea PufferThe Pea Puffer is best kept in a tank of its own kind. Tiny but a persistent fin nipper and hunter. Best in a species-only tank; eats snails and shrimp.
  • Yellow Lab CichlidThe Yellow Lab Cichlid is a Lake Malawi cichlid and belongs in a rift-lake tank with similar cichlids.
