Best Tank Mates for Amano Shrimp

Good tank mates for a amano shrimp include bristlenose plecos, cherry shrimps, chili rasboras, common plecos. Larger than cherry shrimp and a strong algae eater. Does not breed in fresh water.

Adult size2 in (5 cm)
Minimum tank10 gallons (38 L)
Temperature64–82 °F (18–28 °C)
pH6–7.8
TemperamentPeaceful
Group size3 or more
Swims atBottom
DietOmnivore
Care levelEasy
Scientific nameCaridina multidentata

Avoid

  • AxolotlThe Axolotl is best kept in a tank of its own kind. An amphibian that needs cold water. Fish nip its gills, and it eats anything it can fit in its mouth, so keep it alone or with other axolotls.
  • OscarThe Oscar hunts shrimp and will eat amano shrimp.
  • Pea PufferThe Pea Puffer is best kept in a tank of its own kind. Tiny but a persistent fin nipper and hunter. Best in a species-only tank; eats snails and shrimp.
