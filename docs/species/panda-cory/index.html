Avoid

  • AxolotlThe Axolotl is best kept in a tank of its own kind. An amphibian that needs cold water. Fish nip its gills, and it eats anything it can fit in its mouth, so keep it alone or with other axolotls.
  • OscarAn adult Oscar can swallow a Panda Corydoras (about 5 cm), and will.
  • Convict CichlidThe Convict Cichlid is aggressive and will bully or kill a peaceful Panda Corydoras.
  • DiscusNo shared temperature: Panda Corydoras needs 20–25 °C, Discus needs 28–31 °C.
  • German Blue RamNo shared temperature: Panda Corydoras needs 20–25 °C, German Blue Ram needs 27–30 °C.
  • Pea PufferThe Pea Puffer is best kept in a tank of its own kind. Tiny but a persistent fin nipper and hunter. Best in a species-only tank; eats snails and shrimp.
  • Yellow Lab CichlidThe Yellow Lab Cichlid is a Lake Malawi cichlid and belongs in a rift-lake tank with similar cichlids.
