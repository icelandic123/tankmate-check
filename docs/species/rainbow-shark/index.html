Best Tank Mates for Rainbow Sharks

Good tank mates for a rainbow shark include angelfishs, betta fishs, cardinal tetras, dwarf gouramis. Territorial toward bottom dwellers and its own kind; keep only one.

Adult size5.9 in (15 cm)
Minimum tank55 gallons (208 L)
Temperature75–81 °F (24–27 °C)
pH6.5–7.5
TemperamentSemi-aggressive

Avoid

  • AxolotlNo shared temperature: Rainbow Shark needs 24–27 °C, Axolotl needs 16–20 °C.
  • Cherry ShrimpThe Rainbow Shark hunts shrimp and will eat cherry shrimp.
  • White Cloud Mountain MinnowNo shared temperature: Rainbow Shark needs 24–27 °C, White Cloud Mountain Minnow needs 14–22 °C.
  • DiscusNo shared temperature: Rainbow Shark needs 24–27 °C, Discus needs 28–31 °C.
  • Pea PufferThe Pea Puffer is best kept in a tank of its own kind. Tiny but a persistent fin nipper and hunter. Best in a species-only tank; eats snails and shrimp.
  • Red Tail SharkBoth are territorial bottom 'sharks' of the same shape; they fight constantly.
  • Yellow Lab CichlidThe Yellow Lab Cichlid is a Lake Malawi cichlid and belongs in a rift-lake tank with similar cichlids.
