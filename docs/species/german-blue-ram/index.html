Best Tank Mates for German Blue Rams

Good tank mates for a german blue ram include angelfishs, cardinal tetras, ember tetras, clown loachs. A dwarf cichlid that needs warm, soft, clean water. Pairs claim a patch of the bottom.

Adult size2.4 in (6 cm)
Minimum tank20 gallons (76 L)
Temperature81–86 °F (27–30 °C)
pH5–7
TemperamentPeaceful
Group sizeAlone or a pair
Swims atBottom
DietOmnivore
Care levelHard
Scientific nameMikrogeophagus ramirezi
