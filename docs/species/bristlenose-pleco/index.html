Best Tank Mates for Bristlenose Plecos

Good tank mates for a bristlenose pleco include angelfishs, betta fishs, bronze corydoras, cardinal tetras. Stays small for a pleco. Males defend caves from other plecos. Needs driftwood and vegetables.

Adult size4.7 in (12 cm)
Minimum tank30 gallons (114 L)
Temperature70–81 °F (21–27 °C)
pH6.5–7.5
TemperamentPeaceful
Group sizeAlone or a pair
Swims atBottom
DietHerbivore
Care levelEasy
Scientific nameAncistrus spp.

Possible, with caution

  • African Dwarf FrogThe African Dwarf Frog is a slow eater; the faster Bristlenose Pleco will take most of the food unless you target-feed.
  • Fancy GoldfishThe Fancy Goldfish is a slow eater; the faster Bristlenose Pleco will take most of the food unless you target-feed.
  • OscarAn adult bristlenose is armored and usually ignored by an oscar, but a small one can be eaten. Add it only fully grown and give it caves the oscar can't reach.

Avoid

  • AxolotlNo shared temperature: Bristlenose Pleco needs 21–27 °C, Axolotl needs 16–20 °C.
  • Convict CichlidThe Convict Cichlid is aggressive and will bully or kill a peaceful Bristlenose Pleco.
  • DiscusNo shared temperature: Bristlenose Pleco needs 21–27 °C, Discus needs 28–31 °C.
  • Pea PufferThe Pea Puffer is best kept in a tank of its own kind. Tiny but a persistent fin nipper and hunter. Best in a species-only tank; eats snails and shrimp.
  • Yellow Lab CichlidThe Yellow Lab Cichlid is a Lake Malawi cichlid and belongs in a rift-lake tank with similar cichlids.
