Best Tank Mates for Yellow Lab Cichlids

The yellow lab cichlid does best in a tank of its own kind. The mildest mbuna, but still a Lake Malawi cichlid: belongs with other African rift-lake cichlids in hard, alkaline water.

Adult size3.9 in (10 cm)
Minimum tank55 gallons (208 L)
Temperature75–81 °F (24–27 °C)
pH7.6–8.6
TemperamentSemi-aggressive
Group sizeAlone or a pair

Best tank mates for a yellow lab cichlid

None of the species we cover are a safe match. Keep it with its own kind.

Possible, with caution

  • African Dwarf FrogThe African Dwarf Frog is a slow eater; the faster Yellow Lab Cichlid will take most of the food unless you target-feed.
  • Amano ShrimpThe Yellow Lab Cichlid picks at shrimp; adult amano shrimp are big enough to usually survive, but moulting shrimp are at risk.
