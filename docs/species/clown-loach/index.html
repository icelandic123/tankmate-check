Best Tank Mates for Clown Loaches

Good tank mates for a clown loach include angelfishs, betta fishs, bristlenose plecos, cardinal tetras. Grows slowly to around 30 cm and lives for decades. Eats snails eagerly.

Adult size11.8 in (30 cm)
Minimum tank125 gallons (475 L)
Temperature77–86 °F (25–30 °C)
pH6–7.5
TemperamentPeaceful
Group size5 or more
Swims atBottom
DietOmnivore
Care levelModerate
Scientific nameChromobotia macracanthus
