Best Tank Mates for Nerite Snails

Good tank mates for a nerite snail include african dwarf frogs, angelfishs, betta fishs, bristlenose plecos. An excellent algae eater that does not reproduce in fresh water, though it lays hard white eggs.

Adult size1 in (2.5 cm)
Minimum tank5 gallons (19 L)
Temperature72–82 °F (22–28 °C)
pH7–8.5
TemperamentPeaceful
Group sizeAlone or a pair
Swims atAll levels
DietHerbivore
Care levelEasy
Scientific nameNeritina spp.
