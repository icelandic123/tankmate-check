Best Tank Mates for African Dwarf Frogs

Good tank mates for a african dwarf frog include fancy goldfishs, mystery snails, nerite snails. Nearly blind and a slow eater; fast fish often take its food before it finds it.

Adult size2.4 in (6 cm)
Minimum tank10 gallons (38 L)
Temperature72–79 °F (22–26 °C)
pH6.5–7.8
TemperamentPeaceful
Group size2 or more
Swims atBottom
DietCarnivore
Care levelEasy
Scientific nameHymenochirus spp.
