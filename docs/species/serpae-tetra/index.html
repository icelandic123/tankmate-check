Best Tank Mates for Serpae Tetras

Good tank mates for a serpae tetra include bristlenose plecos, bronze corydoras, cardinal tetras, dwarf gouramis. One of the more persistent fin nippers among tetras. A larger group spreads the nipping among themselves.

Adult size1.8 in (4.5 cm)
Minimum tank20 gallons (76 L)
Temperature72–81 °F (22–27 °C)
pH6–7.5
TemperamentSemi-aggressive
Group size6 or more
