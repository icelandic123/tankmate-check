Best Tank Mates for Fancy Goldfish

Good tank mates for a fancy goldfish include african dwarf frogs. A cool-water fish with heavy waste output. Will eat anything that fits in its mouth, including small fish and shrimp. Slow swimmers lose food to fast tankmates.
