A 5-gallon (19 L) tank can hold 1 different community setups that all pass our compatibility checks. Each plan lists a centerpiece, a school, and a bottom or cleanup crew, with the shared temperature and pH that suit every species in it.

Plan 1: Chili Rasbora + Cherry Shrimp

  • 8 × Chili Rasbora (school): One of the smallest aquarium fish; easily eaten or out-competed by bigger tankmates.
  • 10 × Cherry Shrimp (cleanup crew): Breeds readily in a planted tank. Baby shrimp are eaten by almost any fish.

Every pair in this plan checks out as compatible. Shared water: 68–82 °F (20–28 °C), pH 6.5–7.

Before you buy

  • Cycle the tank first. Add fish over several weeks, starting with the hardiest group, so the filter bacteria keep up.
  • The counts are the minimum group sizes. In a 5-gallon tank, stick close to them rather than adding extra species.
  • Quarantine new fish if you can, and watch the first two weeks closely.
